How can we help?

Answers to common questions, or raise a ticket and our team will get back to you.

Frequently asked

How long does installation take?

Once payment is confirmed, a technician visits in your chosen slot, usually within 48 hours in covered areas. The setup itself takes about an hour.

Is my data really unlimited?

Yes. Home and Business packages have no data caps. You pay for speed, not gigabytes.

How do I pay my monthly bill?

Use Mobile Money, card, or the Telesôl app. You'll get a reminder three days before your renewal date.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my package?

Anytime from your account. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at your next renewal.

What is Boafo?

Boafo is our community Wi-Fi at markets, lorry stations and campuses. You buy a short code and connect, with no installation needed. Visit Boafo.
